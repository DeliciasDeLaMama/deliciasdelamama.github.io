    Questions

    How far ahead should I order?

    Order at least 1 day ahead so la Mama can plan the batch. For big orders, message her a few days before.

    Do you deliver?

    Ask la Mama on WhatsApp. She will confirm whether she can reach your suburb and what it costs.

    How do I pay?

    La Mama tells you how to pay when she confirms your order on WhatsApp.

    What about allergies?

    Tell la Mama in your WhatsApp message and she will tell you exactly what goes into each dish.
